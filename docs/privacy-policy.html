2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you sign in with Apple, we receive:

  • A unique Apple User ID (an anonymised identifier Apple assigns you)
  • Your name (only on first sign-in; you can edit what Apple shares)
  • Your email address, which may be your real email or Apple's private relay address — depending on what you choose to share

We store this to create and maintain your account on our servers.

2.2 Reading Data

  • Books you search for and add to your library (title, author, cover image, ISBN)
  • Reading progress and notes you record
  • Chapter numbers or ranges you submit for AI summaries

2.3 AI Usage Data

  • The text of AI-generated summaries and character analyses produced for you
  • Your usage counts (number of summaries generated this month) to enforce free-tier limits and Pro soft caps
  • API cost metadata (tokens used, cost in USD) stored internally for our own financial tracking — never shared with you or third parties in identifiable form

2.4 Subscription and Purchase Data

  • Your subscription tier (free or Pro), status, and expiry date
  • Whether you are a Founding Member
  • A RevenueCat customer ID linked to your account

We do not see or store your payment card details. All payment processing is handled by Apple and RevenueCat.

2.5 Analytics and Diagnostics

  • App events (e.g. screens viewed, features used, errors encountered) collected via PostHog
  • Device type, iOS version, and app version
  • Crash reports via Sentry

Analytics events are associated with a pseudonymous user ID, not your name or email.

2.6 App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

Book Companion does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. We do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Analytics collected via PostHog and crash data via Sentry are scoped to Book Companion only and are not used to build cross-app advertising profiles.

2.6 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • The actual text content of books you read
  • Your precise location
  • Your contacts, photos, or microphone
  • Any data from other apps on your device

5. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
  • AI-generated summaries: Stored until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Usage tracking data: Monthly usage counters reset on the 1st of each month. Aggregate data may be retained for up to 12 months.
  • Analytics: Pseudonymous event data retained for up to 12 months.
  • Crash reports: Retained for up to 90 days.
  • Subscription records: Retained for up to 7 years for accounting and tax compliance purposes.

6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

If you are in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: Ask us to delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restrict processing: Ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at shree.mandlekar@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

7. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time by emailing shree.mandlekar@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account". We will process your request within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Book Companion is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at shree.mandlekar@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

9. Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

  • All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Authentication is handled via Apple's Sign in with Apple — we never store passwords
  • Our backend database (Supabase) uses row-level security to isolate user data
  • API keys and secrets are stored as environment variables and never committed to source code

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Data Transfers

Some of our third-party providers are based in the United States (Anthropic, RevenueCat, PostHog, Sentry, Vercel). When we transfer data outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • The UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (the "UK-US Data Bridge"), where the US recipient is certified under the Data Privacy Framework programme
  • UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs) or Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the UK ICO, where the Data Bridge does not apply

You can obtain further details about the safeguards in place for specific transfers by contacting us at shree.mandlekar@gmail.com.
